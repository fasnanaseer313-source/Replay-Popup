WHAT WE SET UP

Three ways to experience RePlay.

Each setup is tailored to your space and crowd. From tight sprint tracks to rugged terrain → we build the experience around you.

Racing Tracks

High-speed RC cars on custom sprint and circuit tracks. Built for head-to-head races, time trials, and pure adrenaline. The crowd goes loud.

Crawling & Obstacle Courses

Technical, slow-paced, and incredibly satisfying. RC crawlers navigate realistic terrain → rocks, ramps, bridges. Precision over speed.

Custom Setups

Got a theme or a space? We customise. Branded tracks, themed obstacles, competition formats → the setup works around your event, not the other way.
